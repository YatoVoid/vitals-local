Language check

Runs the app in one language after another and reports how much of the text actually changed. Untranslated counts the strings that came back identical to the English. A few are expected: product names, single letters, and anything a language borrows unchanged. A large number means that language is not really working.

Languages marked "pick by hand" have an on-device model that has not been fetched yet. The browser only starts that download during a real tap, and one tap is worth one model, so open the language screen, choose that language once, then run this again.
